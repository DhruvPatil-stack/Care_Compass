heart failure discharge instructions - synthetic

SYNTHETIC PATIENT FIVE / mrn 100-005-001

your diagnosis: heart failure (congestive heart failure, CHF)

MEDICATIONS -- weigh yourself EVERY morning before eating. call doctor if you gain more than 2 lbs in a day or 5 lbs in a week.

metoprolol succinate 50mg daily (morning, with food)
lisinopril 5 mg po once daily
furosemide (lasix) 40mg po every morning -- this is a water pill; you may urinate more
spironolactone 25mg daily
warfarin 5mg daily -- INR must be checked regularly
potassium chloride 20 mEq PO BID (replace what furosemide removes)
digoxin 0.125mg PO daily

follow up:
cardiology within 5 days of discharge. this is critical.
primary care in 2 weeks

restrictions:
FLUID RESTRICTION: no more than 2 liters of fluid per day (includes soups, juice, ice cream)
low sodium diet: limit to 2000 mg sodium per day
no vigorous exercise; walk slowly for 5-10 minutes daily as tolerated

wound care: not applicable

return precautions:
call 911 for: sudden severe SOB, chest pain radiating to arm or jaw, loss of consciousness
call doctor for: weight gain > 2 lbs/day, increased swelling in ankles, worsening SOB
