DISCHARGE SUMMARY -- POST-OPERATIVE KNEE REPLACEMENT
SYNTHETIC TEST CASE ONLY

Patient information: Synthetic Patient Four | 100-004-001
Surgeon: Synthetic Surgeon

Operation: Left total knee replacement

Medications
Take all of the following exactly as prescribed:
- OxyCODONE 5mg oral every 4-6 hours as needed for SEVERE pain (do not drive while taking)
- Acetaminophen (Tylenol) 1000mg by mouth every 6 hours scheduled (take even if not in pain)
- Aspirin 325mg PO TWICE DAILY for blood clot prevention for 6 weeks
- Celecoxib 200mg PO daily for inflammation
- Docusate (Colace) 100mg PO BID while taking oxycodone (prevents constipation)
- Enoxaparin (Lovenox) 40mg SC once daily -- blood clot prevention injection

Follow-up Appointments
Orthopedic surgery clinic: 2 weeks post-op (call 555-0100 to confirm)
Physical therapy: 3 times per week starting 2 days from discharge

Activity
- NO weight bearing on left leg for 48 hours, then partial weight bearing with walker
- No driving for 6 weeks
- No lifting greater than 10 lbs for 6 weeks
- Use walker at all times when ambulating

Diet
Regular diet. Eat high-protein foods to help healing.

Wound Care
- Keep incision dry for 7 days
- No showering until staples removed at follow-up
- Change dressing daily; call if redness, warmth, or drainage increases

Return Precautions
CALL 911 or go to ER immediately if:
- Sudden severe shortness of breath
- Chest pain
- Calf pain or swelling (may indicate DVT)
- Fever over 101.5
- Incision opens, drains pus
