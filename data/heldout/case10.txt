INPATIENT DISCHARGE - SEPSIS RECOVERY
SYNTHETIC PATIENT TEN | ID: 100-010-001

You were treated for sepsis (serious infection in your blood). You are going home on oral antibiotics to finish your treatment.

PLEASE TAKE THE FOLLOWING MEDICATIONS:

Levofloxacin 750 mg oral once daily for 7 days [ANTIBIOTIC - complete the full course]
Acetaminophen (Tylenol) 650 mg oral every 6 hours as needed for fever or body aches (max 4 doses/day)
Sertraline 50 mg oral daily in the morning [continue your regular antidepressant]
Omeprazole 40 mg oral once daily before breakfast

SCHEDULE THESE VISITS:
1. Your primary care provider ASAP - within 3 days if possible.
2. Infectious disease clinic - 2 weeks.

ACTIVITY AND REST:
You may feel very tired for 2-4 weeks after sepsis. This is normal. Rest when needed.
Increase activity gradually. Short walks are good.
No return to work for at least 1 week.

DIET:
Eat well. High protein foods help recovery. Stay well hydrated.

WOUND CARE:
Central line site: keep dry for 72 hours.

GO BACK TO THE ER IF YOU HAVE:
temperature over 101F, shaking chills, confusion, decreased urination, rash.
