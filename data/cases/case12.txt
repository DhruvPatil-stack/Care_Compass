DISCHARGE SUMMARY
HARD CASE: MIXED MEDICATIONS + COMPLEX WOUND CARE
SYNTHETIC PATIENT TWELVE (MRN: 100-012-001)
DIAGNOSIS: Cellulitis, right lower extremity; Uncontrolled Hypertension

MEDICATIONS:
1. Cephalexin (Keflex) 500 mg PO four times daily for 10 days -- antibiotic for skin infection
2. Amlodipine 10 mg by mouth once daily
3. Losartan 50 mg oral once daily
4. Hydroxychloroquine (Plaquenil) 200 mg oral twice daily -- take with food

FOLLOW-UP:
Infectious disease or wound care clinic: 3-5 days to recheck leg.
Primary care: 2 weeks.

ACTIVITY:
Elevate right leg above heart level when sitting or lying down (pillows under leg).
Limit walking to necessary activities only for 1 week.
No strenuous activity for 2 weeks.

WOUND CARE:
Wound on right lower leg:
- Clean gently with mild soap and water once daily.
- Apply mupirocin ointment to wound twice daily.
- Cover with clean dry gauze.
- Do NOT use hydrogen peroxide or iodine.
- Mark the border of the redness with pen; return to ER if it spreads past the line.

DIET: Low sodium; stay hydrated.

WHEN TO RETURN:
- Spreading redness past the marked line
- Red streaks going up the leg
- Fever over 101F
- Severe pain, numbness, or cold foot
