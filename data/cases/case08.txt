Discharge Instructions Following Appendectomy
Synthetic Hospital - Synthetic Case 8

Name: Synthetic Patient Eight  MRN: 100-008-001

Your procedure: Laparoscopic appendectomy (appendix removal through small cuts in abdomen)

Medications to take at home:
- Ibuprofen (Advil/Motrin) 600 mg by mouth every 8 hours WITH FOOD for 5 days
- Oxycodone/Acetaminophen (Percocet) 5/325 mg 1-2 tablets by mouth every 4-6 hours as needed for severe pain
- Ondansetron (Zofran) 4 mg PO every 8 hours as needed for nausea
- Docusate sodium (Colace) 100 mg by mouth twice daily while taking oxycodone

Follow-up:
General surgery clinic in 2 weeks for wound check and staple removal.
Call the surgery office within 24 hours to schedule.

Activity:
No lifting more than 15 pounds for 4 weeks.
No driving while taking oxycodone.
You may shower after 48 hours. Keep incision clean and dry.
Walking is encouraged starting the day after surgery.

Wound Care:
You have 3 small incisions closed with staples.
Keep covered with clean dry gauze for 48 hours.
After 48 hours remove the bandages and shower; pat dry.
No bathing, pools, or hot tubs until staples are removed.
Watch for: increasing redness, warmth, swelling, or yellow/green drainage.

Diet:
Start with clear liquids and advance to regular diet as tolerated.
Avoid fried and spicy foods for 1 week.

Return to Emergency Department if:
- Fever greater than 101 degrees F
- Severe abdominal pain not relieved by medication
- Vomiting that prevents you from taking medication
- Wound separates or has foul-smelling drainage
