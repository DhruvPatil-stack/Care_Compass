DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS
Synthetic Patient Nine | MRN 100-009-001
Diagnosis: Atrial Fibrillation, new onset

Your doctor has started you on blood thinners and heart medications. It is very important to take these as directed.

Medications:
Apixaban (Eliquis) 5 mg PO BID -- blood thinner. Do NOT skip doses.
Metoprolol tartrate 25 mg PO BID -- controls heart rate
Digoxin 0.125 mg PO daily -- helps heart beat regularly (take at same time each day)
Atorvastatin (Lipitor) 40 mg PO qHS
Aspirin 81 mg PO daily

Follow-up:
Cardiology: within 5 days. Very important.
Primary care doctor: 2 weeks.

Activity:
No strenuous exertion until cleared by cardiologist.
Light walking encouraged.

Diet:
Heart healthy diet: low sodium, limit alcohol to zero.
Avoid grapefruit and grapefruit juice (interacts with some medications).

Wound care: none required.

CALL 911 OR GO TO ER: sudden severe shortness of breath, chest pain, stroke symptoms (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty), palpitations that feel like heart racing uncontrollably.
